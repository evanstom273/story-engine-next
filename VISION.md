# Story Engine Next Vision

Story Engine Next is a persistent narrative engine for long-form
storytelling.

It is not a chatbot, novel generator or generic AI workspace. It exists
to help authors build living fictional worlds whose continuity survives
across chapters, stories and years.

## Goals

-   Long-form storytelling
-   Persistent canon
-   Reusable universes and characters
-   AI as collaborator, never replacement
-   Cross-device writing
-   Fast, immersive writing

Every feature must ultimately improve storytelling.
